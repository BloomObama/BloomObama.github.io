# Admissions-policy review — October 3, 2026

Scope: international first-year undergraduate admission, 2026–27 application cycle. This batch adds 12 reviewed profiles to the previous 46. Institutions are joined by exact IPEDS ID, not by a similar name. Each published field retains its own official source in `college-policies.js`; runtime profiles are generated from that editorial layer.

Reviewed fields: international financial aid, SAT/ACT, English proficiency and waiver conditions, admission application fee, and admission/aid deadlines. Full demonstrated need is an institution's calculation, not a guaranteed full scholarship. Published general rules do not automatically cover transfer students, combined professional programs or auditions.
