English testing is required without three years of fully English-medium secondary instruction; bilingual instruction does not qualify for this exemption. Про заклад

A public institution in Irvine, California, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 29,500 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a large-city setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is social sciences, followed by psychology. Federal designations reported for the institution include Hispanic-Serving Institution; Asian American and Native American Pacific Islander-Serving Institution.
