Фінансова допомога

International applicants are automatically reviewed for the $43,000 Count Folke Bernadotte Scholarship, not guaranteed a full ride. Admission requires proof of ability to pay at least $33,800/year through certified sponsor/parent bank documents. Campus employment is separately evaluated. The catalog also requires ISFAA; confirm package, renewal and future costs.
