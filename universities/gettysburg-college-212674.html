Фінансова допомога

International need-based aid is competitive and limited; Gettysburg strives to meet need but does not state a universal guarantee. Full-tuition grants are possible but uncommon; loans, employment and family contribution may be included. All applicants submit the International Student Financial Statement; aid seekers also submit the separate international aid form, not CSS Profile. Break housing/food and mandatory health insurance are additional costs. Admission and aid pages list different round deadlines: confirm them with the international office.

02

SAT / ACT

SAT/ACT are strongly recommended on the international page but are not required. Self-reported scores can be submitted and are verified upon enrollment. English-proficiency requirements are separate.

03

Англійська мова

The international page requires proficiency testing if classroom instruction has not been English for at least two years, or English is not the first language. Ask admissions to confirm waiver eligibility rather than assuming English-medium schooling alone suffices. TOEFL 90, IELTS 7.0 and DET 120 are competitive guidance, not minimum application cutoffs. Official scores may come from an agency or school official; no English-test superscoring. The page has not published a new-scale TOEFL conversion.

04

Application fee

Gettysburg's current admissions FAQ lists a $60 application fee. Qualified applicants may request a fee waiver through their admissions counselor. International applicants follow the same Common Application process, with additional international documents where applicable.

Дедлайни

International first-year application deadlines: Early Decision I November 1, Early Action November 15, Early Decision II January 20, and Regular Decision January 15. The ED agreement is a separate supporting document: November 1 for EDI and January 15 for EDII. International financial forms are due by the chosen application deadline.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Gettysburg, Pennsylvania, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 2,200 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a town near an urban area. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is social sciences, followed by biological sciences.
