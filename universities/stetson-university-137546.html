Фінансова допомога

International applicants are automatically considered for Stetson scholarships after admission and may separately apply for premier merit programs. Need-based aid may not be available to international students, and U.S. federal aid is unavailable. The university requires a Certification of Finances and proof of one year's funding before issuing the I-20; scholarship awards count toward that total.

Англійська мова

Applicants who are neither from an English-speaking country nor educated at an English-speaking school for at least three years must prove proficiency. Accepted minimums are TOEFL 4.0 on the new 1–6 scale (approximately the previous 79), TOEFL Essentials 9, IELTS 6.0 or Duolingo 95; completing ELS Intensive English 112 is also accepted. Official scores must come directly from the testing agency.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in DeLand, Florida, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 2,300 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a small-city setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is business and management, followed by visual and performing arts.
