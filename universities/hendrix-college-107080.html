International admission is need-sensitive. Plan to demonstrate at least $35,000 yearly contribution, described as the average gap after scholarship, not a guaranteed net price. Submit CSS; if unavailable, request Hendrix ISFAA from admissions. No universal full-need/full-ride promise is approved.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Conway, Arkansas, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 1,100 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a small-city setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is biological sciences, followed by social sciences.
