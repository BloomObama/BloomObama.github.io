Фінансова допомога

International applicants are automatically considered for merit scholarships and need-based assistance after completing their application. Full scholarships are unavailable; a personal contribution is required. Submit Lawrence's own Certification of Finance and supporting documents within five days of the admission deadline; CSS Profile cannot replace it. Awards renew at the same level subject to the published terms.

Англійська мова

Proficiency evidence is required. Accepted thresholds include IELTS 6.5, DET 115, TOEFL iBT 80 before January 21, 2026 or 4.0 thereafter, SAT EBRW 580, or ACT English/Writing 24. English as the primary language or school-official confirmation of English-medium high school can establish proficiency. Test agencies must send scores directly; student/counselor copies are not accepted.

Дедлайни

First-year fall applications: ED November 1, EA December 1, ED II and RD January 15; later applications are also accepted. Certification of Finance is due within five days of the application deadline. The page is not entry-year labelled. Conservatory applicants need an audition and separate round guidance; do not infer their eligibility from this general schedule.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Appleton, Wisconsin, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 1,400 students. Its reported awards extend through the bachelor's level, and it has its main campus in a small-city setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is visual and performing arts, followed by biological sciences.
