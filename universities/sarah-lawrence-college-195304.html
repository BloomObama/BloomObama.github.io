Фінансова допомога

International merit/need scholarships are highly competitive, usually $10,000–$25,000/year, renewable subject to satisfactory academic progress. This is not a full-cost guarantee. Complete the free SLC International Student Financial Aid Application through the portal and supply translated family income evidence. Forms become available 2–3 business days after Common App; receipt within ten days of the application deadline has no adverse impact. Failure to complete aid requirements makes students ineligible for funding during all four years.

Англійська мова

Applicants whose first language is not English and who have not studied in an English-language curriculum for the past four years should submit TOEFL, IELTS or Duolingo. Competitive scores (not stated as universal minimums): TOEFL 100 before January 21, 2026, or 5–5.5 afterwards; IELTS 7+; DET 130+. Extensions may be requested for hardship.

Application fee

Sarah Lawrence's own Common App profile lists no first-year application fee. This concerns undergraduate application, not fees for master's programmes, early childhood education or study abroad.

Дедлайни

First-year EA and ED I applications November 1; ED II and RD January 15. ED is binding and requires parent/counsellor signatures. Optional interview deadlines are November 10 for EA/ED I, January 19 for ED II and January 31 for RD. International aid forms follow those rounds with the ten-day document window on the international aid page. Generic FAFSA instructions apply only to eligible applicants, not ordinary international aid applications. No entry year is labelled.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Bronxville, New York, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 1,450 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a large-suburb setting. Its largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is liberal arts and general studies.
