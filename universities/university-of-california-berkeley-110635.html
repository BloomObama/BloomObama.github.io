International students cannot receive federal, state or university need-based aid. Berkeley's admissions office points to scholarship and external funding resources, but does not promise full funding for international applicants.

Англійська мова

First-year English proof: TOEFL 90 on the old scale/4.5 on the new scale, IELTS 6.5 or DET 115. Qualifying AP/IB exams or at least three years of exclusively English instruction can also satisfy it. Mixed-language schooling does not qualify for that exemption. Berkeley lists January 31 as its proficiency completion deadline.

Про заклад

A public institution in Berkeley, California, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 33,000 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a midsize-city setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is social sciences, followed by computer and information sciences. Federal designations reported for the institution include Asian American and Native American Pacific Islander-Serving Institution.
