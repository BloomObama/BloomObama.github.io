Фінансова допомога

International admission is need-aware: Centre says limited finances can prevent admission. Highly qualified international applicants may receive limited need-based grants and are automatically considered for annual merit scholarships of $20,000–$45,000. Full need-based funding is not offered. The separately applied-for Lincoln Scholars Program is the only full-funding route and covers tuition, fees, room and board plus wraparound funds. Every international applicant must submit the Certificate of Finance; Centre does not require CSS Profile or a separate aid application.

SAT / ACT

SAT or ACT scores are optional for international first-year applicants. English-proficiency testing is a separate requirement and is not replaced by the test-optional policy.

03

Англійська мова

International applicants must provide English proficiency unless Centre approves a curriculum-based waiver. Published minimums are TOEFL iBT 93, TOEFL Essentials 9.5, IELTS 7.0, Pearson PTE 64 or Duolingo 125. Applicants completing an English-language IB or Cambridge A-Level curriculum may request a waiver, which is evaluated only after the other application materials arrive.

Application fee

Centre's international first-year application through the Common Application is free. This statement covers the college application fee, not English testing, credential translation, visa, travel, enrollment or other costs.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Danville, Kentucky, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 1,350 students. Its reported awards extend through the bachelor's level, and it has its main campus in a distant-town setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is social sciences, followed by biological sciences.
