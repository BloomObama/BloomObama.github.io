Фінансова допомога

Augustana College in Rock Island, Illinois offers automatic merit consideration and case-by-case international need-based aid through its free-standing international aid application in the applicant portal. Do not apply domestic FAFSA requirements or the South Dakota university's policies. Admission considers ability to meet costs; no universal full-need/full-cost guarantee is stated. A private nonprofit institution in Rock Island, Illinois, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 2,400 students.
