Фінансова допомога

International merit/need aid is available, not universally full-cost. Non-UWC grants/merit can cover tuition, leaving roughly $15,000 of room/board beyond aid; UWC/Davis funding can reach the comprehensive fee. Loans and earned campus wages are not grants. Need-based applicants may use CSS (6638), free ISFAA or Intl ACAC; no international CSS fee waivers. Merit-only applicants need no aid form; fine-arts awards require separate applications. Both biological parents' finances are considered. Renewal requires academic progress and grants may not track all cost increases; budget insurance, travel and other indirect expenses separately.

SAT / ACT

International SAT/ACT are optional. Self-reported results are accepted for admission review; enrolled students who submitted scores must provide official reports. English evidence and major-specific music/nursing requirements remain separate.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Northfield, Minnesota, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 3,000 students. Its reported awards extend through the bachelor's level, and it has its main campus in a distant-town setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is social sciences, followed by visual and performing arts.
