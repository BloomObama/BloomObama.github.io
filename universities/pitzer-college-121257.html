Фінансова допомога

International applicants seeking need-based assistance submit Pitzer's PIFAA after the Common Application, not CSS Profile or FAFSA. The international page says the 2027–28 form is being updated and applicants will be contacted. Published aid priority dates: ED I November 15; ED II/RD January 12. These differ from admission deadlines.

SAT / ACT

Pitzer does not accept SAT/ACT for admission through the 2026–27 application cycle: this is test-free, not merely optional. English-language testing remains a separate requirement.

03

Англійська мова

Non-native speakers submit official TOEFL iBT, IELTS or Duolingo results, or receive a waiver. Standard thresholds: TOEFL 5.5 (105 on the pre-January-2026 scale), IELTS 7.5 or DET 135. Lower qualifying bands require extra English coursework; the separate International Scholars pathway has different thresholds.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Claremont, California, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 1,200 students. Its reported awards extend through the bachelor's level, and it has its main campus in a large-suburb setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is social sciences, followed by natural resources and conservation.
