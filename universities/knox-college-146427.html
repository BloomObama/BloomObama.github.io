Фінансова допомога

Knox offers scholarships but does not offer need-based aid to international students. All applicants submit an Affidavit of Support proving sufficient funding. Need-aware admission is stated separately on the international application page; do not interpret a scholarship as full cost coverage.

Англійська мова

English proficiency is required: IELTS 6.5, TOEFL iBT 80 (4.5 for tests after January 2026), or DET 115. Scores must remain valid within two years. Exemptions may cover two consecutive full-time study years in the US/an English-only official-language country, four secondary years at an American-accredited English-medium school abroad, or official AP English Language 4+, IB English Language and Literature 5+, or A/AS English C+. Predicted scores and English-medium instruction alone do not qualify.

Дедлайни

International first-year deadlines: binding ED and nonbinding EA I November 1; EA II December 1; RD January 15. Later applications are considered on a space-available rolling basis. Supporting documents, including arts submissions, must meet the applicable deadline. The source does not label an entry year; reconfirm dates before submitting.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Galesburg, Illinois, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 1,000 students. Its reported awards extend through the bachelor's level, and it has its main campus in a distant-town setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is social sciences, followed by biological sciences.
