Фінансова допомога

International applicants can receive both need-based and merit aid. Need-based support requires a separate financial-aid application by the admission deadline. Enrolling students must certify resources for costs not covered by aid before an I-20 is issued. No automatic full-funding promise is made here.

02

SAT / ACT

SAT/ACT are optional for all undergraduate applicants, including international students. Under Bennington's no-harm policy submitted results are used only when they strengthen admission chances. Self-reported scores are accepted during application; official copies are requested on enrollment.

03

Англійська мова

English testing is required unless English is native or at least three secondary-school years were taught fully in English. TOEFL, IELTS, DET and PTE Academic are accepted. There is no fixed minimum; typical successful results are TOEFL 100/5.0, IELTS 7, DET 130 or PTE 70. These are guidance, not guaranteed admission thresholds.

Application fee

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Bennington, Vermont, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 750 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a distant-town setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is visual and performing arts, followed by English language and literature.
