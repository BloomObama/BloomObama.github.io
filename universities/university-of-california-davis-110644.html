Про заклад

A public institution in Davis, California, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 32,000 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a small-suburb setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is biological sciences, followed by social sciences. Federal designations reported for the institution include Asian American and Native American Pacific Islander-Serving Institution.
