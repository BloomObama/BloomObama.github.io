01

Фінансова допомога

International first-year applicants are automatically considered for academic merit scholarships. Limited need-based support requires the International Student Financial Aid Application; applying early is encouraged. Drew Action Scholars and arts awards require additional applications. Full-cost funding is not promised.

02

SAT / ACT

SAT/ACT submission is optional, including for merit scholarship consideration. Applicants choose on their application. Drew superscores SAT but uses the highest single ACT composite rather than an ACT superscore.

03

Англійська мова

Non-native English speakers need proof of proficiency. Accepted exams include TOEFL iBT/Essentials, IELTS, Duolingo, PTE Academic and English GaoKao. Individual waivers may be granted for SAT/ACT results or previous study in an English-speaking country. The pathway program is separate from direct admission.

04

Application fee

The regular undergraduate application has a $40 non-refundable fee. The separate Tilghman International Center pathway advertises no application fee; that offer is not the regular undergraduate fee policy.

Дедлайни

Published first-year deadlines: ED I November 1; EA December 1; ED II January 15; RD February 1, then space-available review. Spring deadline January 2. These are the current general deadlines; the table does not specify an entry year. Apply early for limited need-based aid.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Madison, New Jersey, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 1,500 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a large-suburb setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is social sciences, followed by business and management.
