Фінансова допомога

Beloit's new All-In guarantee applies to incoming full-time Fall 2027 international students who complete admission and aid applications by January 15, 2027. Gift aid caps 2027–28 tuition, double-room housing, meals and fees at $30,000; it does not promise full need or zero cost. International aid forms accepted: Beloit's form, CSS Profile or International ACAC form. Insurance, travel, books, personal expenses, summer courses and non-exchange study-away are excluded; single rooms cost extra. Scholarships/grants are guaranteed for eight semesters, but annual tuition increases of 2–5% may raise later costs above $30,000. Lower-income average net prices include loans and are not individual guarantees.

02

SAT / ACT

Beloit remains SAT/ACT-optional and considers highest subscores if submitted. International English proficiency is separate; SAT/ACT may be one way to meet it. Recommendations, portfolios and interviews are not compulsory in the current review process.

03

Англійська мова

International applicants must demonstrate English proficiency. Published minimums: DET 115, IELTS 6.5, TOEFL iBT 80 before January 21, 2026 or 4.0 with every subscore at least 4.0 afterwards; confirm tests taken on the transition date. TOEFL Essentials is accepted without a numerical threshold on this page. SAT reading/writing 580 or ACT English/Writing 24 are alternatives. Native English or English-medium education can support a requested waiver; the page states at least three years of instruction. Requests are reviewed after application and transcripts, not automatically approved.

04

Application fee

Beloit's own Common App profile lists no first-year application fee. Fees for external English exams, translation or CSS Profile are separate and must not be assumed waived.

Дедлайни

First-year EA I November 1, EA II December 1, RD January 15; all rounds are non-binding and non-restrictive. The All-In guarantee explicitly requires both complete admission and aid applications by January 15, 2027 for Fall 2027 entrants. International aid uses an accepted international form, not the domestic FAFSA instruction in the general deadline table. Transfer dates are separate. Contact admissions about crisis-related deadline flexibility rather than assuming an extension.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Beloit, Wisconsin, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 900 students. Its reported awards extend through the bachelor's level, and it has its main campus in a small-city setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is social sciences, followed by biological sciences.
