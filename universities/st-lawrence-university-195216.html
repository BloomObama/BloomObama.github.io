Фінансова допомога

International merit and need-based aid is competitive. Indicate aid intent on the application and submit Certificate of Finance/documentation together; CSS Profile and ISFAA are not required. Merit awards range from $5,000 to full tuition, not a general full-cost guarantee. Davis UWC matriculants have a separate full-demonstrated-need commitment excluding travel. Two Kenya-specific scholarships cover tuition/fees/room/board but not books, personal expenses or transport; they are not open to every nationality. CoF priority is February 1, later review depends on funding. The Kenya instructions still mention discontinued SAT subject tests: confirm directly.

SAT / ACT

St. Lawrence explicitly makes SAT/ACT optional for all applicants. This does not remove required English proficiency evidence. Дедлайни

The international schedule lists ED I November 2, EA December 1, ED II February 1 and RD March 1, without an entry year. Financial-aid CoF priority is separately February 1. The official consumer policy at https://www.stlawu.edu/consumer-information/admissions-policies says international applicants needing aid who are not Canadian should apply RD, so confirm round eligibility before choosing ED. The direct audit collector was denied access to that consumer page; it was read through official-page web results, not counted as a fresh captured source.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Canton, New York, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 2,000 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a remote-town setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is social sciences, followed by psychology.
