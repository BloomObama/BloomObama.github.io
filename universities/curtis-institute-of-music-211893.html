Фінансова допомога

Admitted students receive merit-based full-tuition scholarships without a separate aid application. Living-expense assistance may be considered separately; tuition-free does not mean all costs are covered.

SAT / ACT

SAT is optional for Bachelor of Music applicants. Admission is audition-based; academic/degree placement is evaluated after acceptance.

03

Англійська мова

English evidence is required after acceptance for degree placement, not to submit the application. TOEFL iBT 4 (older 79–80), IELTS Academic 6.5–7.0 per area (confirm the required value), or Cambridge 169 are listed. Home tests are not accepted. Three years at an accredited in-person English-medium secondary school can support an exemption request. Insufficient proficiency may lead to Diploma rather than bachelor's placement.

Application fee

$250 nonrefundable application fee plus $50 for a live audition; late materials incur $75 extra. Applicants of any nationality can request a significant-financial-need waiver through the application.

Дедлайни

Application/prescreening December 10; waiver request December 3. Undergraduate reply May 1; official transcripts/test scores July 15. Recommendations/unofficial transcripts: December 31 per https://www.curtis.edu/apply/faq/. The general schedule is not entry-year labelled; confirm instrument openings/auditions.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 100 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a large-city setting. Its largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is visual and performing arts.
