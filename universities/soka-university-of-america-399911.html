Фінансова допомога

International undergraduates can apply for institutional need-based aid using Soka's International Student Financial Aid Application. Grants, scholarships and institutional loans are distinct aid types; loans are optional and not free funding. Apply by February 15 for priority review, no later than March 2; verify requested financial documents in the Learn portal. This page does not establish a universal full-cost award or need-blind admission policy.

SAT / ACT

SAT/ACT are optional for undergraduate admission. Submitted scores must be official and no older than five years; SAT Subject Tests are not required.

03

Англійська мова

Non-native speakers demonstrate proficiency with TOEFL or DET. Preferred thresholds are TOEFL iBT 4 (80 before January 2026) or DET 115. IELTS and Cambridge English are not accepted. Official agency scores are required, within two years; self-reports are not accepted. These are undergraduate rules, not the graduate program's separate requirements.

Application fee

$30 undergraduate application fee. Soka accepts Common Application, College Board and NACAC fee waivers; submit an eligible waiver or payment to complete the application.

Дедлайни

Undergraduate application and documents: nonbinding EA November 1; Regular Admission January 15. Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Aliso Viejo, California, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 450 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a large-suburb setting. Its largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is liberal arts and general studies.
