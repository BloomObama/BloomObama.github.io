Фінансова допомога

Divine Word College says its financial-aid coordinator discusses funding plans with international students and that several scholarships are available; it does not state that every international degree applicant receives full-cost aid. The separate seminary scholarship and inability-to-pay promise in the college FAQ are specifically for Divine Word Missionary priest/brother candidates, not all international or lay students. Federal work-study on this page is limited to qualifying domestic students. The 2026–27 full-time tuition listed here is $8,400 per semester, before room, board and other fees; applicants should request an individual aid offer.

02

SAT / ACT

The college's admissions FAQ says SAT/ACT scores are not required. Applicants who have taken either exam are asked to submit their results; the degree-seeking application checklist likewise requests results of any standardized tests taken. English-proficiency assessment is a separate requirement for non-native English speakers entering the undergraduate program.

03

Англійська мова

For full-time undergraduate degree study, a non-native English speaker must provide TOEFL or an equivalent English-test score, or take Divine Word College's own English-proficiency test before placement. The published admissions criteria do not specify a numeric minimum on this page. Intensive English Language Institute applicants are a distinct non-degree route and should not be treated as automatically admitted to an undergraduate degree.

04

Application fee

The current degree-seeking application checklist requires a $25 application fee for full- or part-time degree applicants, including international applicants. The separate tuition-and-fees page calls this application fee non-refundable; no automatic fee waiver is stated. Do not confuse this with the additional SEVIS/I-20 administrative charge shown on the cost page.

05

Дедлайни

The college's live admissions FAQ says international applications are processed until June 15 for fall entry and November 1 for spring entry; domestic dates differ. The FAQ does not label those dates with a specific intake year, so applicants should confirm the applicable 2026–27 term and document-arrival timing with admissions before relying on them. Applications are encouraged as early as possible, and the committee normally reviews a complete file within two weeks.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Epworth, Iowa, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 60 students. Its reported awards extend through the bachelor's level, and it has its main campus in a distant rural area. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is theology and religious vocations, followed by philosophy and religious studies.
