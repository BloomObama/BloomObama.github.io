Фінансова допомога

International first-year applicants can seek RISD institutional, need-based funding through the aid form released in the admissions portal after submitting the application. The Fall 2027 aid-form deadlines are November 15, 2026 for early decision and January 25, 2027 for regular decision. RISD explicitly says awards should not be assumed to meet full demonstrated need. Institutional funding is level-funded through the normal program length with proportional tuition-increase adjustments. Non-U.S. citizens use RISD's institutional form; domestic FAFSA instructions and federal grants or loans are not applied to them.

02

SAT / ACT

All first-year applicants, domestic and international, may apply without SAT or ACT scores by selecting the test-optional option in the RISD Common Application. Submitted SAT/ACT results can be superscored. For applicants who speak English as a second language, a separate proficiency requirement remains unless an approved English-medium-school waiver applies; SAT/ACT scores can also be used to meet the specified English thresholds.

03

Англійська мова

Applicants who speak English as a second language, including U.S. citizens, must document proficiency. RISD lists new-scale TOEFL 5 (previously 93), IELTS 7 or Duolingo 120; SAT Reading/Writing 600 or ACT English and Reading 25 are also accepted. English-medium secondary education can qualify for a requested waiver only after three consecutive full-time years without ESL/ELL/ENL courses; the waiver form appears in the applicant portal after submission. A waiver is not automatic. IELTS UKVI General is not accepted.

04

Application fee

RISD's 2027 first-year application page lists a nonrefundable $60 Common Application charge and a separate nonrefundable $10 SlideRoom portfolio submission fee. A qualified applicant can request a Common Application fee waiver or email admissions for a manual waiver if the standard categories do not fit. The page does not explicitly promise that the portfolio fee is covered by that waiver, so ask RISD rather than assuming it disappears.

05

Дедлайни

For the Fall 2027 first-year cycle, the admission application is due November 1, 2026 for binding early decision or January 20, 2027 for regular decision. The separate institutional aid forms are due November 15, 2026 or January 25, 2027 respectively. The portfolio is required as part of the first-year application, and the Brown|RISD dual-degree route has an additional Brown application and separate dates. Graduate and transfer aid dates are not first-year deadlines.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Providence, Rhode Island, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 2,100 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a midsize-city setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is visual and performing arts, followed by interdisciplinary studies.
