Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 1,900 students. Its reported awards extend through the bachelor's level, and it has its main campus in a small-city setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is interdisciplinary studies, followed by social sciences.
