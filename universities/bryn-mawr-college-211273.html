Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Bryn Mawr, Pennsylvania, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 1,350 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a large-suburb setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is social sciences, followed by biological sciences. It is designated as a women's institution.
