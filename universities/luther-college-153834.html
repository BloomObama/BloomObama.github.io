Фінансова допомога

International aid combines merit, need-based support and campus employment, but resources are limited; the page says families often contribute half or more of expenses. Its aspiration to meet need is not a universal full-need guarantee. Automatic academic merit can reach $39,000/year and is renewable through year four. Submit signed/verified ISAFA and supporting bank/wage documentation, not domestic FAFSA. A campus job is guaranteed to willing international participants with estimated annual earnings of $3,800, not an upfront grant. Some talent or external awards need separate materials/deadlines; their dates are not general admission deadlines. Confirm the individual package and remaining costs.

Англійська мова

Non-native speakers who do not attend English-speaking high school must submit TOEFL, IELTS or DET. Minimums: older TOEFL 80 before January 21, 2026; new TOEFL 4 after that date; Essentials 8.5, IELTS 6.5, DET 115. Confirm exams taken exactly on the transition date. US college-credit/composition alternatives listed for transfers are not automatically first-year waivers. Submit original/certified academic records and complete English translations.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Decorah, Iowa, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 1,450 students. Its reported awards extend through the bachelor's level, and it has its main campus in a remote-town setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is visual and performing arts, followed by health professions.
