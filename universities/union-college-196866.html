Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Schenectady, New York, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 2,100 students. Its reported awards extend through the bachelor's level, and it has its main campus in a small-city setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is social sciences, followed by engineering.
