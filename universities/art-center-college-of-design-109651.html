01

Фінансова допомога

ArtCenter says international entrants may be considered for institutional scholarships by indicating interest in aid/scholarships on the admission application; a separate FAFSA is not required or available to them. Awards depend on portfolio/academic merit, available funds and committee review, and the college describes merit-only international awards as limited. It does not promise a full scholarship or full-cost coverage. Scholarship priority dates are February 1 for fall and October 1 for spring, but admission may continue on a rolling basis for majors with open places. Domestic federal/state grants and loan rules are not copied to international applicants.

02

SAT / ACT

SAT or ACT results are optional for undergraduate applicants, including students applying directly from high school. Portfolio and program-specific essays remain required, and English-language testing may be required separately for applicants without an English-medium high-school or bachelor's degree.

03

Англійська мова

Applicants without a high-school diploma or bachelor's degree taught in English must submit TOEFL iBT, Academic IELTS or Duolingo English Test results. Current posted minimums are new-scale TOEFL iBT 4, IELTS 6.5 or DET 115. Results must come directly from the test agency, be less than two years old, and not be an institutional test. ArtCenter may request testing/interview even after English-medium study if proficiency is in doubt or ESL coursework was taken. Older handbook values of TOEFL 80 and DET 105 are superseded by the current admissions page; no conversion is invented.

04

Application fee

The current undergraduate online application has a nonrefundable $50 fee. Applicants may seek a College Board waiver or submit a guidance-counselor/teacher letter to admissions for consideration; approval is not automatic. The much older paper form's separate international fee is not applied to the current online process.

05

Дедлайни

For Fall 2027, ArtCenter lists an Early Action deadline of November 15 and a regular priority date of February 1. Most undergraduate majors use rolling admission after the priority date until the major fills. Entertainment Design is the exception: its priority deadline is February 1 for fall and October 1 for spring, and the department decides whether to keep accepting applications afterward.

Про заклад

A private nonprofit institution in Pasadena, California, with an undergraduate enrollment of about 2,100 students. Its reported awards extend through the graduate level, and it has its main campus in a midsize-city setting. The largest reported field by share of awarded degrees is visual and performing arts, followed by engineering technologies.
